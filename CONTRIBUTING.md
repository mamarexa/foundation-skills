# Contributing

Thanks for helping make feasibility studies better for every founder.

## What helps most

1. **Benchmarks with sources.** Industry ratios, startup-cost ranges, wage and employer-burden
   rates, rent per m², or tax rates for a country or city. Add them to the relevant
   `references/benchmarks.md` with a link to the source and the year.
2. **Corrections.** A skill step that's wrong or out of date, or that professionals do
   differently. Explain how it's done in practice.
3. **Test cases.** A realistic business description plus what a good study for it should
   contain. These become evaluation cases for the skills.

## Rules

- Every number needs a source you'd be comfortable showing a lender.
- No personal data, and no confidential or paid-report content you don't have the right to
  share.
- Keep each `SKILL.md` under about 300 lines. Put detail in `references/`.
- Don't add instructions that make an agent calculate financial results itself. The engine does
  the math (see [CONVENTIONS.md](CONVENTIONS.md)).
